Cape Town

A taste of what a week could look like

Seven days.
Plenty to remember.

This is an example, not a package. The best itinerary depends on who is coming, how much you want to move around and what your family actually enjoys.

Build ours ↗
Day 1

Arrive in Cape Town

Settle in, take the temperature of the city and start with a dinner that suits the flight you have just had.

Day 2

Cape Town, properly

Mountain, neighbourhoods, a good lunch and enough room to change the plan when the weather does.

Day 3

The Peninsula

Chapman's Peak, Cape Point, coast and one or two stops worth getting out of the car for.

Day 4

Into the Winelands

Move towards Stellenbosch or Somerset West. One estate, a long lunch, pool or golf later in the day.

Day 5

Stellenbosch

Food, wine, gardens or art. Pick two things you will remember rather than five things you will forget.

Day 6

Franschhoek

A beautiful drive, a serious lunch and time to browse galleries, shops or simply stay put.

Day 7

Your family's day

Beach. Golf. Hiking. Padel. Wine. Food. Or nothing at all. The trip should fit you, not the other way around.
